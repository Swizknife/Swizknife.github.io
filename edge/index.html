Design decisions
  • The mic gets its own core.A camera upload can block loop() for 100 ms or more. The audio task on core 0 keeps filling the PSRAM buffer meanwhile, so no samples are lost.
  • Drop new audio rather than block the mic.If the socket falls behind and the 2 s buffer fills, new frames are counted and dropped. The buffer is flushed on reconnect so stale speech is never replayed.
  • One socket for audio and commands.Each TLS session costs ~40–50 KB of internal RAM, so audio rides the command WebSocket instead of opening a third connection.
  • The newest frame wins.CAMERA_GRAB_LATEST with two buffers on the ESP32 and a drop-oldest queue on the server mean nobody works on a stale image.
  • Two allow-lists.The server only sends known commands, and the firmware ignores anything it doesn't recognise.
